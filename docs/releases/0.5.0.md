1943 joins as the fourth core: jotego's jt1943, upright or rotated, with RetroAchievements.

> **Hardware:** a Sipeed Tang Nano 20K and a **Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W**. The firmware runs on the Pico 2 W only.

## Download

- `game20k-0.5.0-pico2w.zip`: the firmware for the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W.
- `game20k-0.5.0-tangnano20k.zip`: one image for the Tang Nano 20K's flash with the Galaga, Pac-Man, 1942 and 1943 cores.

Setup as in the [README](https://github.com/scullymi/game20k/blob/v0.5.0/README.md#quick-start) and `FLASHING.txt`. For 1943, put the MAME set `1943` into `roms/`.

## Changes since 0.4.0

- 1943: The Battle of Midway (Capcom) on jotego's jt1943 core, with its achievements on RetroAchievements, reachable through the core switch.
- The ROM in the SDRAM is read as a stream with several reads in flight. 1943 fetches its scroll tiles and map words ahead, so the picture has no wrong tile segments.
- WiFi after a restart: the Pico 2 W lets its WiFi chip repeat an unanswered authentication and leaves the access point before a restart of its own, so it joins at the first attempt.
- The clock falls back to `pool.ntp.org` when neither `config.ini` nor DHCP names an NTP server, and every NTP server from `config.ini` is used.
- `config.ini` lines may be up to 126 characters long.
- `scripts/make_rom.py` finds a chip by its SHA-1 when another MAME release names it differently.
