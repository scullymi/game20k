Pang and Super Pang with all their achievements, and a cleaner sound on the Pac-Man core.

> **Hardware:** a Sipeed Tang Nano 20K and a **Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W**. The firmware runs on the Pico 2 W only.

## Download

- `game20k-0.6.1-pico2w.zip`: the firmware for the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W.
- `game20k-0.6.1-tangnano20k.zip`: one image for the Tang Nano 20K's flash with all eight cores.

Setup as in the [README](https://github.com/scullymi/game20k/blob/v0.6.1/README.md#quick-start) and `FLASHING.txt`. The ROM files on the SD card stay as they are.

## Changes since 0.6.0

- Pang, Super Pang, Buster Bros. and Super Buster Bros. load their whole achievement sets and no longer drop to softcore. The firmware drops the parts of a set it does not need as the set arrives and holds up to 128 achievements.
- Pac-Man, Ms. Pac-Man and Jr. Pac-Man: each voice of the sound chip swings around its centre, as in MAME, so short notes no longer thump. Jr. Pac-Man still has a buzz in the game, see Known issues in [docs/status.md](https://github.com/scullymi/game20k/blob/v0.6.1/docs/status.md).
- The README is a short page with pictures. The details are in [docs/games.md](https://github.com/scullymi/game20k/blob/v0.6.1/docs/games.md), [docs/retroachievements.md](https://github.com/scullymi/game20k/blob/v0.6.1/docs/retroachievements.md) and [docs/status.md](https://github.com/scullymi/game20k/blob/v0.6.1/docs/status.md).
- docs/games.md explains Pang's test menu: Pang and Buster Bros. confirm there with start 2, which has no button by default.
