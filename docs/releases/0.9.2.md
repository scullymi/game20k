game20k now also runs without a Pico: on the Tang Nano 20K alone or with an M0S Dock.

> **Hardware:** a Sipeed Tang Nano 20K with a **Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W**, with an **M0S Dock**, or alone. RetroAchievements need the Pico 2 W.

## Download

The zips now start with `cores-` or `firmware-`:

- `game20k-0.9.2-cores-tangnano20k.zip`: the cores for a Pico 2 W or an M0S Dock on the header.
- `game20k-0.9.2-cores-tangnano20k-singleboard.zip`: the cores for the Nano alone.
- `game20k-0.9.2-firmware-pico2w.zip`: the firmware for the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W.
- `game20k-0.9.2-firmware-bl616.zip`: the firmware for the Nano's own BL616 (board revisions 3923 and 3921) and for the M0S Dock.

Setup with a Pico as in the [README](https://github.com/scullymi/game20k/blob/v0.9.2/README.md#quick-start), without one as in [docs/bl616.md](https://github.com/scullymi/game20k/blob/v0.9.2/docs/bl616.md). The SD card of 0.9.1 keeps working.

The images for the M0S Dock and for board revision 3921 are untested so far, feedback is welcome in #1.

## Changes since 0.9.1

- The Companion firmware runs on a BL616: the Tang Nano 20K's own, with cores built for it, or an M0S Dock with the normal cores. Game switch and menus as with the Pico, without RetroAchievements (#1).
- The firmware is based on the current upstream FPGA-Companion and TinyUSB.
- Everything builds with free tools on macOS, Linux and Windows through `g20k.py`, with the same files on each, see [docs/BUILD.md](https://github.com/scullymi/game20k/blob/v0.9.2/docs/BUILD.md).
